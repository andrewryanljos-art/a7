Accessibility Statement

Handbag Nova aims to make its content usable across desktop and mobile devices and with common assistive technologies.

Design approach

We use readable typography, meaningful headings, visible links, responsive layouts and sufficient contrast as core design goals. Interactive controls are intended to remain usable at different screen sizes.

Motion

Animations are kept purposeful and the site should respect reduced-motion preferences where supported by the browser.

Images

Content images include alternative text where the image contributes meaningful information. Decorative visual treatments should not be required to understand the page.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, contact us at the address or phone number listed on the Contact page. Please describe the page, the task you were trying to complete and the problem you experienced so we can investigate it.
