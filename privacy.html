Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Handbag Nova may collect, use and protect information when you browse the website, contact us, subscribe to communications or interact with our content.

Information we may receive

Depending on how you use the site, information may include your name, email address, contact details, messages you send, subscription preferences, device/browser information and basic website usage data. We do not ask you to provide sensitive personal information merely to browse the editorial site.

How we use information

We may use information to respond to enquiries, deliver requested newsletters, maintain website security, understand aggregate site usage, improve content and comply with applicable legal obligations. We do not needlessly retain information for purposes unrelated to the service you requested.

Sharing and service providers

We may use hosting, analytics, email, security and technical service providers to operate the website. These providers should receive only information reasonably required for their service. We do not represent that every third-party service has identical privacy practices, so visitors should review the policies of services they choose to interact with.

Retention and security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information from unauthorized access, loss or misuse. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the stated purpose, legal requirements or legitimate business records.

Your choices

You may request access, correction or deletion of personal information where applicable. You may also unsubscribe from marketing or editorial emails using the available unsubscribe mechanism. Some operational records may need to be retained where required by law or legitimate security needs.

Children

The site is not intentionally directed to children, and we do not knowingly request personal information from children for marketing purposes.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when the website, services or legal requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page.
