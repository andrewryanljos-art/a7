Style / The quiet outfit anchor

A handbag can be the element that turns simple clothing into a complete look.

The first consideration

Start with a restrained base: clean trousers, a shirt, a knit or a simple coat. Then let the bag provide the shape, texture or color story.

Make the decision fit your life

The most convincing accessory styling feels intentional without looking overworked.

Nova note

Trends can be useful for inspiration, but longevity comes from choosing a silhouette, material and scale that continue to make sense after the season changes.
