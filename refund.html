Refund Policy

This Refund Policy explains the general approach to refunds for purchases connected to Handbag Nova. If a third-party seller processes the transaction, its current refund policy and applicable consumer law govern the purchase.

Eligibility

Refund eligibility can depend on the product, seller, purchase date, condition and applicable law. Items may need to be unused, in original condition and accompanied by packaging or proof of purchase where required.

Requesting a refund

Contact the seller through the channel shown on your order confirmation and provide the order reference, item details and reason for the request. Do not send an item to an address that has not been authorized.

Inspection

Returned goods may be inspected before a refund is approved. Items showing use, damage, missing components or signs of alteration may be subject to deductions or may be ineligible where legally permitted.

Refund timing

Once approved, refunds are normally sent to the original payment method. Bank or card processing times are outside our control and may require several business days.

Exceptions

Certain personalized, final-sale, hygiene-sensitive or otherwise restricted products may have different refund rules where permitted by law.

Consumer rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to remove rights that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable consumer-protection legislation.
