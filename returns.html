Returns & Exchanges

We want purchases associated with Handbag Nova to be considered and straightforward. The following guidance applies unless the seller's product-specific terms or applicable law provide different rights.

Before returning

Keep the item in its original condition and retain packaging, tags and proof of purchase until you are satisfied with the item. Contact the seller before shipping a return.

Condition

Returned items should generally be unused, unwashed, unaltered and free from avoidable damage. Handbags should be protected from scratches, moisture, makeup transfer and strong odors during the return period.

Exchanges

An exchange may depend on stock availability. If the requested replacement is unavailable, the seller may offer a refund or another available remedy.

Return shipping

Whether return shipping is paid by the customer or seller depends on the reason for the return and the seller's stated policy. Faulty, incorrect or damaged items may receive different treatment.

Refund after return

After the return is received and inspected, the seller will determine the appropriate refund or exchange in accordance with its policy and applicable law.

Important

Do not return products to the street address listed on this website unless the seller specifically instructs you to do so.
