Shipping Policy

This page provides general shipping guidance for orders or partner purchases associated with Handbag Nova. Where a third-party retailer fulfills an order, that retailer's current shipping terms take precedence.

Processing

Orders are generally processed after payment and any required order verification. Processing times can vary according to product availability, weekends, holidays and seller handling procedures.

Delivery estimates

Delivery windows shown at checkout or by the relevant seller are estimates rather than guarantees. Remote areas, weather, customs, carrier disruptions and seasonal demand can extend delivery times.

Tracking

Where tracking is available, a carrier tracking reference may be supplied after dispatch. Tracking updates are controlled by the carrier and may take time to appear after a parcel enters the network.

International shipments

International deliveries may be subject to customs procedures, duties, taxes or import restrictions. Unless the seller states otherwise, these charges are generally the responsibility of the recipient.

Address accuracy

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate delivery address. Delays or additional charges caused by an incorrect address may not qualify for free replacement shipping.

Damaged or missing parcels

If a parcel arrives visibly damaged, document the condition and contact the relevant seller or carrier promptly. For a parcel that appears delivered but cannot be located, check the delivery area and contact the carrier before opening a formal claim.
